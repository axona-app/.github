Axona is a desktop app for your own knowledge base. A shortcut opens a capture window over whatever is on screen, and search finds the note again across the whole folder. Your notes stay plain text files on your own disk, in Markdown or Podlite. The typed graph and agents are in development.

- **[Download Axona for macOS](https://github.com/axona-app/releases/releases/latest)** — Apple Silicon and Intel.
- **[Discussions](https://github.com/axona-app/releases/discussions)** — questions, ideas, and feedback.
